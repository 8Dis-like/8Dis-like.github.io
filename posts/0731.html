Amusing Ourselves to Death

大道行思
元智慧
发布日期:   2026-07-23
更新日期:   2026-07-31

Amusing Ourselves to Death: Public Discourse in the Age of Show Business is a prophetic 1985 book written by educator and media theorist Neil Postman. The central premise argues that the rise of television has transformed public discourse into a form of entertainment, reshaping serious arenas like politics, journalism, religion, and education into superficial spectacles. Rather than being oppressed by a top-down authoritarian regime, Postman warns that modern society willingly pacifies itself through an endless appetite for distractions. Though published decades ago to critique television, the work has taken on even greater significance in the internet and smartphone era. Modern social media algorithms, short-form video feeds, and gamified content perfectly fulfill Postman’s warnings about a society “scrolling itself to death” and trading deep cognitive focus for constant hits of amusement.

Admittedly Postman analyzed and predicted exorbitantly on the trendy modern society, his insights diminish in reflecting the core of amusement. Personally, the title applies even more broadly. “衣食住行娱” are always the essential factors of our human society. Amusement seems inferior to others but I think it still holds there since all work and no play makes Jack a dull boy. To some extent, it’s amusement that injects the randomness of the whole system to derive innovation from the stable oriented balance struck by the other 4 factors. Once innovation appears, it got tested, sifted, improved, and expanded into the other 4 factors. Amusement could also combine, wire, and even redefine the other 4 factors, catering our greedy demand until death.

Living in the capital of amusement, Los Angeles for 1 year as a observer. Certain long-lasting puzzles clear out in my mind just like LA’s iconic smog. And now I am writing to drop some thoughts, trying to piece them together organically.

Nature Sense

The object of amusement is human. Therefore, it has to satisfy human senses. On the one hand, devices evolve to the zenith gauged by human senses. On the other hand, we strive to cultivate our senses in higher level, naming arts as we know. Common senses endow us capability to share, inspire, feedback and run the amusing loop completely.

The reason I precedes “sense” by “nature” doesn’t just mean “natural sense”. Mother Nature itself is a special sense we human echo. Perhaps due to the primitive environment we left millenniums ago, we always remember mother nature and imprint it into our hearts. We decorate handcrafts with plants. We tame animals into pets. We fake our senses to immerse ourselves in mother nature. Even though we can’t indeed retreat back to the hug of mother nature without any assistance of our tools, we spare efforts to admire, tour, and enjoy the harmony triggered by the hidden shortcuts to connect with mother nature.

In fact, nature sense is a underlying decisive variable in human society. Land with better scenery is labeled higher price. Artworks make surplus with more exposure to mother nature. It is never absent from any tourism of any form, either. This is one reason why LA is so unique. Mountains, beaches, sea, desert, islands all squeeze into this 464 sq miles bounded area with always sunny weather and cozy temperature. As long as the water supply is secured and wildfire risk is capped, it’s hard to lose in many aspects in terms of city competition. Luckily, leveraging its exclusive advantages, LA breeds and leads the superior amusement all over the world.

However, nature sense itself is not enough, otherwise it’s too lonely for human to endure. Modern nature adventures such as surfing, kayaking, skiing, hiking, biking, parasailing, camping, or picnic, neither of them could be done solo as leisure or be done with partners as amusement. Since there’s necessity to share experiences, there has to be methods to organize people together. One and the best way is story group.

Story Group

Ever since we started to cooperate, we started to build community. It stands on our common memories, beliefs, and stories. It bonds us in groups under unified topics. A good example is right here: Hollywood. It is born as an Eden for emerging shooting and filming practices thanks to its diverse background and geographical isolation. It unites guilds of directors, actors, playwriters, and players for similar causes. It implements Joseph Cambell’s theory: The Hero With a thousand faces to industrialize spirit narrative. It shapes itself the summit of movie arts by lavish ceremony like Oscar.

Frankly speaking, Hollywood is not crown as it used to be anymore. But the legacy persists as runes. It treats live itself as a show, composing of spots to form stories. Edited with artistry, everything could be reorganized behind curtains and presented vividly on stages. Audience is also part of the show as they resonate themselves with the subtle emotions been conveyed. Inheriting from ancient Greece, it’s civilian’s obligation to attend theaters to engage with the society. It turns out rooting deep into our social structure. Regardless of the generality, Ancient Rome bath culture becomes sauna nowadays. Colosseum becomes stadium. People group together exchanging the acknowledgement, creating the vibe, and enjoying the sense of belongings.

Heading towards modern ages, variants are popping up everyday but the core remains the same. Concert tours, talk shows, 剧本杀…… If someone succeeds in catching eyes, the community mechanism could kick off the symbiosis of star and fandom. Equipped with modern technologies, both sides seem always-online intensifying interactions and group identity. South Korea is the master of this. Benefiting from the small territory and acute aesthetic, they quickly tune up each corner into the Internet, tune in tournament hierarchically to choose the best, and tune on exports fashion as idols.

Nevertheless, story and groups could rise, but they still need commercial operations to reach outside, churning out the spin of the between business and culture.

Commercialization

When it comes to business, Americans are always the best. As the backbone of capitalism, everything could be priced. Seldom you catch a cultural phenomenon out of no reason. Usually there’s a whole system operating behind the scene just like celebrity agents. Whether personal daily habits or team’s publicity stunt, it could be branded as attachments or developed as derivatives. Underwriters liquidate and amortize IP franchise. Brokers water stocks and stimulate cults. Speculators manipulate dynamical price in World Cup. Audience only wait for the show, whilist businessmen invite audience to join the show.

Although commerce tends to introduce chaos into amusement via naive market mechanism, it calls for deep-pocket organizations to unify under consensus, which marks the capability of technology. As we human scale up to play, so does the supporting system. We exploit computing resources and devise various architectures to tailor situations, trading off between concurrency, accessibility, and appearance. We launch matches and tournaments to wrap up trophy with dreams. We pollinate traditions with emerging trends to form new courtesy. Nonetheless, the cores penetrating through souls are always stable but profound values reflecting ages and wisdoms.

Where there’s business, there’s brutal battle, and there’s winner and loser. Taking advantage of information asymmetry or patent monopoly, profits could be lucrative. Sheets could be gorgeous. Game might get out of order in mania. Giants may utilise funds to the full to underpin growth ad undermine or acquire competitors. Stealth demonstrate their venality by attempting to take credit for someone else’s inventions, thwarting pure tech or public ambitions.

Human desire is like a boulder rolling down a high mountain; once set in motion, it becomes unstoppable.

So, how do we on earth amuse ourselves to death today?

As far as I am concerned, the principle should be human-centric while the key is time. Without human, the GDP number itself makes no sense. Considering human entertainment, the GDP number could be severely bloated. In that case, we are not obligated to obsess with any index. More broadly speaking, don’t take any rules set by others for granted cause the users are just monkeys for experiments. Life itself could be treated as a game and there’s no fixed pattern to follow.

Time value varies a lot for different subjects. In recent years, there exists premium as a membership. Theoretically, pure service may charge at will as long as deal. Hence I suggest just consume at will, as amusement itself is totally non-essential, it’s unnecessary to bargain on margins or rush in advertisements in sacrifice of additional mind costs, which implicitly betray the original intention of amusement. Adjusted by the market and empirically, cost discounts often means service discounts. Afterall, 买家没有卖家精。Moreover, rather than struggling to degrade other’s time value, it’s more rewarding to elevate our own personal time value, which would boost consumption demand amplifying economy.

久利之事勿为，众争之地勿往 ——曾国藩

To conclude, we’d better pivot ourselves aligned with the macroeconomic flows mentioned above. At the end of the day, we want to amuse ourselves to death easily, freely, and happily.
