财商

大道行思
元智慧
发布日期:   2026-09-18
更新日期:   2026-09-29
文章字数:   2.9k
唯一词数:   2012
阅读时长:   11 分

近日顺利入职Bridger Investment Partners，在落实计算机技术理论的同时跟进了解业务需求。坦白讲，作为门外汉我并没有太深厚的金融功底，但既然进入了这一行，多少也开始锻炼出些许对账本数字的sense。至于本篇标题则来源于近日集中突击学习吴军最新的财商训练课。关于财富，我在一年多前攥写了一篇名为Fortune的文章，但那篇更像是在我首次打造金融认知系统之后从学生视角的梳理，成为了一篇融合各家冲突观点并非系统架构的大杂烩。本篇则是在我正式融入社会分工协作网络后从现代公民视角对金钱财富话题的总结与智慧提炼，也会自然引导出我的原则风格与实操引线。

依据吴军所言，财商是一个人的系统性的能力，是其生活态度的延申，包含理解财富、获取财富、增值财富和使用财富等多个维度。整体来看，财商是独立于智商情商对人的另一评判指标。不同于智商决定智力的天花板，或是情商限制领导力的极限，财商则是一种现代社会中特有的以金钱为媒介衡量个人智慧的尺度。下面，我就从三方面展开分享我个人对财商的见解心得。

识数

一般而言，人的生活被定义在自然数范围，对于大数，人多大数并不敏感。在入职Bridger每日淹没在Excel与金融公式后，我对此深有体会。为了提升对大数的敏感度与精细度，会计发明了间隔符号，只不过不同文明具有不同的形式，西方使用三位对应thousand, million, billion，中国则使用四位对应万/亿。当然，两者都作用于方便记账的实际由印度人发明的阿拉伯数字，相比之下，气场更足的汉字与罗马数字则继续留存于场面之上。

在生活中，数字可能更多出现在广告上，看似随意的版面背后实际是摸透了观众主观情绪的设计，充当一个交易的入口。而在入口之后的账本上，数字就是纹理，刻画了每一个金融实体的完整特性。你对它褒也好，贬也好，它就是在那，如同一栋建筑，可能会老化可能会翻新，但除了地震等意外灾害，它就在那里。既然我们不会对一栋建筑的存在抱有太多主观偏见而只是静静欣赏，那为什么不能同等看待五花八门的金融产品呢？

在说服或论证的过程中，数据常被作为有力的论据，这是因为数字本身就有很强的客观性。一旦基本数量与计算方式达成一致，按照规则正确推导的结果必然一致，而数字在特定场景下被赋予清晰的含义即成为有价值的数据。即使人想做刻意地引导或隐瞒，也很难偏离人们对数学神圣的客观性。事实上，现实中的很多事情尤其是商业行为看似反直觉，实际都是由事件背后极其冷静冷酷的数字逻辑所驱动。无论是财报造假，股价操控，资本运作，杠杆期权，乃至庞氏骗局，其背后都有充分的数据理论作支撑。历史与现实已经无数次印证，只有人服从数字逻辑行事，而非数字为人的意志代言。

长远

From a normal perspective, value is simply the marked price. However, from a financial perspective, time is also a component of value; in a healthy modern economy, the value of money depreciates over time. Consequently, 100 yuan today is more valuable than 100 yuan in the future. When performing quantitative valuation, the system sets initial factor values based on empirical data to run rational model simulations, and calculates the average Net Present Value (NPV) using methods such as Annualized Constant Yields (ACY).

Apart from underlying time value, we need to consider further about hidden costs or risks such as liquidity, Bid-ask spread(negatively correlated with liquidity), transaction costs, and the costs to just hold and maintain it like real estates. Since it’s hard to bet against the time and price, we need just avoid some mistakes like Charlie Munger.

  1. Do not expect to acquire high-quality assets at rock-bottom prices.

  2. No matter how good an asset is, do not buy it if the price is too high.

  3. Strive to trade in markets characterized by robust competition.

Taken together, these principles turn financial judgment into a loop: identify where an asset’s value comes from—its use, operating cash flow, market consensus, or shared belief—then test its liquidity, costs, risks, and price against that source. Wealth is not simply earned; it must also be preserved, deployed, and grown. That means distinguishing realized cash flow from paper gains, counting the full cost of owning an asset, and asking whether a purchase increases the resources I can actually mobilize. On the income side, I should favor work that compounds my skills and earning per hour, rather than a side pursuit that drains the focus needed for my main work. Even if I am planning to change a job or an industry, I should start from the common grounds between the old and the new to secure a smooth transition. Most importantly, I should resist spending before wealth has accumulated, avoid investments I cannot explain, and treat an attractive return as incomplete until I understand its downside. Understanding, acquiring, using, and growing wealth are not separate tricks but links in one system; a failure at any link can undo the rest.

In the long run, wealth grows. However, it is not populated evenly. Fairly speaking, the US has been maintaining the most sophisticated financial system, which efficiently tracks and manages most wealth in the world. The dedicatedly designed index ETFs almost represents the whole market temperature. If you zoom in and zoom out to observe the pattern, you could find out that most gaining happen at several single days that can’t be predicated in advance at all. Therefore, rather than picking the best time to get in or out, just put in the money regularly and forget about it, let money begets money, it will compound and not let you down in a long run. However, the stock market won’t make you rich; at best, it preserves the value of your capital against inflation. To build real wealth, you need to establish a healthy, perpetual cash flow.

标尺

财富的主要载体资产常被评估为一个数字，自然容易成为一把标尺。但实际上，资产的绝对值并不容易衡量，单说资产的形式就有若干种：使用型资产，经营型资产，共识驱动型资产，信仰驱动型资产。每种资产又有各式各样的估值方法。但有一点是确定的，在如今这个信息流高度通畅的时代，很多估值方法的标尺都是动态的。因此，擅长数字游戏的玩家往往在同时使用若干标尺的场景中遵循着一套隐秘的规则。身处其中，不易察觉，最好的做法就是在身处其中前就先根据数学冷静梳理各种可能性以及相应的应对策略。诚然，面板上的数据确实复杂，但其映射的却是更加复杂的现实情形，而很多时候，现实中的因素难以甚至无法被精确建模，例如大众情绪/社会效益等。因此，一把稳定的标尺需要有成熟的金融市场共识去共同维护，尺子有浮动可以，但要把尺子折断，大家都会受损失，迁移到别的尺子去。

在商业社会中，还有一把隐藏但强大的标尺，就是信用，成熟的金融体系如美国市场有FICO等指标作为相对客观的信用分数。但除去专业人士，大部分人在做日常的财务决定时，对信用的评级主要还是靠感觉，感觉其言语是否投机，感觉其理念是否一致，感觉其名望和过往经验是否正紧。但财富不会跟着感觉走，金融的纹理都藏在真实的数据里，要真正做出理性的决定，还是得想办法深入分析一线原始的数据。

若用这些标尺丈量日常，所谓高财商并不是频繁交易，而是形成一套不被二手数字和情绪牵着走的习惯：评估资产时先看它能调动多少真实资源、价值从何而来；做决定前写下机会成本，不把尚未落袋的收益当作可花的钱，并把维护、时间和交易等长期成本一并算入。定期辨别哪些资产在持续创造现金流、哪些其实在消耗资源；把一部分收入和精力投向教育、技能、健康等能够复利的自身能力，对听不懂的投资保持警惕，遇到看似难得的机会先冷静，并尽量选择信息透明、竞争充分的市场。收入增加时不轻易降低储蓄比例，守住现金储备，逐步减少对单一收入来源的依赖；投资时关注长期优势，给复利足够时间，也不因短期迟缓轻易改弦易辙。最后，复盘重大财务决策、预估退出代价，并定期检查资产、负债与现金流。它们共同把分散的选择连成一套更可持续的决策系统。

Just mentioning, financial tools today are extremely abundant and keeping innovating out of your imagination. No matter it’s bonds, stocks, securities, mortgage, or any other term in glossary. Your balance sheet just adds more items on both sides, which makes the system more coupling and less stable. Neither owing money to others nor having others owe money to you is a pleasant experience due to the frictional costs; yet, as I mentioned earlier, free financial operations serves as a catalyst for economic growth. Since such interactions cannot be entirely eliminated, they require specialized management methods—methods that embody a deeper wisdom.

依我所见，钱只是表象，本质是深藏其后的智慧。财富的本质 是调动社会资源的能力。而所谓的财务自由 不过是以自身为节点，构建一套稳定的社会资源调度机制以通过劳动的协同产生正价值的系统，这套系统的财务特征反映到金融预言中 能够容纳各生产要素的波动并相对稳定地产生正收益——个人的安全感依托于这样良好的系统便成为了自由。

另外提一点，财务自由所带来的只是行动灵活性上的自由，并不是行为上毫无约束的完全自由。“有钱能使鬼推磨”，财富本身没有立场，但如何使用财富难免会有立场。尽管可以约束所有者委托职业经理人专门打理财富，我们也应该号召充分发挥财富的本质，调动社会资源做对社会整体有益的事情，留取身前生后名。而不是如历史上大多数达官显贵一样，只是将财富过了手，还给了社会。

最后，以财商训练课的财富回报地图作结，配上我一些凝练的个人思考：不同资产的长期回报可能相差很大，持有时间也会改变我们对收益的判断；而即使方向看对，若在上涨时贪婪、下跌时恐慌，频繁进出，仍可能错过长期回报。历史数据可以作为比较资产、理解复利的标尺，却不是未来的承诺，更不是可以照单全收的投资答案。For me, the next step is not to chase a specific figure or a shortcut to riches. Instead, it is about truly understanding the financial realities of my work and enhancing my ability to generate value and cash flow, while maintaining a lifestyle of disciplined saving, prudent decision-making, and regular self-reflection. Only by transforming knowledge into habit—and then building those habits into a stable system for resource allocation—can wealth evolve from mere numbers on a ledger into robust options, ultimately serving as the foundation for true freedom.
