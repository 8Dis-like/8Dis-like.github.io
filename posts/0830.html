美国游记

While keeping watch from across the ocean—far from the motherland—the Earth has completed one full orbit around the Sun along the ecliptic. Right before graduating, departing, and returning, it is natural to write a piece serving as a ceremonial summary. After much deliberation, I settled on this title: simple, yet general enough to capture the essence of the experience. Although living in the country for 1 whole year, it’s relentless to call here home yet. Instead, why not treat the whole live as a journey, which accounts for the feasibility of the title, like a kaleidoscope encamposing my daily rountines, acute thoughts, deep reflection, and maybe unintended predictions.

Special as this journey is, the structure of this article could be different from previous Travelogue, which appears facts heavy. This article otherwise is intended more flexible. During the last year, I have written about various topics such as business, enterprise, academy, culture, city, friend, love… only to find something left out: life itself. Hence this time I focus on lives, life of myself, lives of others, and life on earth.

Live In

As a 1-year program international student, I don’t have a car. This saves quite a lot of costs and troubles but extremely limits my living range. Typically, I am always within walking distance centering my water-power shaggy apartment. Taking advantage of the proximity to UCLA campus, I tried to attend different activities from hextrogenous organizations spanning arts party, seminars, movies, spring sing, covering each corner to piece together each side of studying aboard. I seldom cater others for a ride. On one hand, this is due to my previous lessons from last summer school experiences. On the other hand, it’s relucant for me to play hard before hunting for a job hard. Therefore, I value every chance to explore outside and usually prepare much in advance for a comprehensive plan sparing flexibility for accidents. This paradiagm is carried out successfully and scaled up gradually, especially after my awareness of the bruin pass on public transits paid by our normal tuition and later resurrection of an ex-roomate legacy but half-assemblied bycicle. Equiped with fruits, snacks, and pre-heat breads, I imaged myself as commissioners centuries ago mapping uncharted areas. I only shop targeted foods partly because of frugality and American’s disadvantage in tastes. Sticking to the faint principles above, I have covered half of LA seashore from Malibu all the way to Hermosa beach and upper-class regions from Bretwood to downtown (only part of it is filled with homeless, mainly came from south or central LA). Right before my departure, I reached the final destination I missed 2 years ago—Griffith Observatory, enjoying my last glimpse of Los Angeles!

You may infer my contempt to local foods from my words above, not surprise, I cook myself most of the time. Thanks to LA’s diversity and inclusion, I could try receipes all over the world such as curry, Sukiyaki, kimchi, burritos, casserole, milkshake, Lasagna, Fatijia, shawarma, turkey… I am proud to say, if I could satisfy my picky appetite well, I should be decent to treat in family dinner. Driven by my geographical tuition, I only hunt for the missing delicacy that I can’t make elegantly myself like Hawaiian Royale, Spanish Paella, Millet Crepe… Besides, I rate side dishes as Ceasar salad dressings, hazelnut spread, Buffalo ribeye, Brazil nuts, Quebec blueberries, Kvass, avacado toast… I intentionally avoid Chinese foods since I cook them everyday and poor value for money except hotpot soup base.

Luckily, Westwood isn’t big and I can walk to Target, Trade Joe’s, Ralphs, and Whole Foods conveniently. There are many e-commerce services discount for students as well. Weee and Amazon are my top-notch suppliers. As for others, I just try for once. Yami for Chinese instant foods, Instcart for immediate delivery, Uber Eats and Doordash for emergency only. As for Bruincard, I strictly capped my consumption, exploiting the 10 bucks left in 2024 for some USA made UCLA stickers. I collected newspapers, magazines, even mail letters, reading them as part of American stylish life. After all, most innovative tech was born out of business advertisements including telephone, television, and search engine. For online apps, I just leave it for my girfriend to explore and learn English interactively via FaceTime, which often leads to unexpected topics. Immersed in Hollywood, I devote my first American film watching to Oddesey in a century-old theater invested by Christophor Nolan.

During the short time window between I landing a job and UCLA capstone presentation, I paid a visit to my cousin on east coast. As a PhD from Maryland University, he lives in a more typically American style, backing up many plates and utensils, only kicking off the dish washing machine when there reaches certain amount. Since I dedicate all my vacation with him, I witness this period as part of my life, too. Driving a second-hand car, we can extends our traces to all DMV(WashingtonDC, Maryland, Virginia) area. Rushing for a popular restaurant, golf game, rountine grocery, exclusive sight seeing beside Chesapeake Bay (my first sight of the Atlantic Ocean), or just casual walking at National Harbour. But except for his free time, I took metros connecting the great DC area myself, which are pretty impressive compared to LA metro. Operating in travel mode, I shrink to 2 meals a day, which aligns well with my cousin’s menu: hamburgers/sandwiches, French Fries, with coke combo. Namely, he along with his roomamate is in working status, thus sharing daily calendars occassionally. We set off together several times for local seafood specialties: blue crab and crab cake. In general, this period serves as both the US capital travel and US working life. The former is magnificant, glorified at the 250 anniversry celebrations. Compared to Beijing, the capital of the USA is less forbidden. Beaux-arts architecures are laid out neatly dotted with bronze or copper statuses. Employees wearing gray suits moved through the streets and buildings closed for renovation (White house is also closed for visitors in the summer, no chance to visit Donald Trump). While the latter is relatively lonely, costy, and homogenous (like a 3A game rendering with fixed NPC passing by every time) despite the freedom compared to working in China. Perhaps it’s also why they co-parenting a Maltese dog and always to act together to hedge this loneliness.

One thing shooks me a lot after this transcontinental travel is my attitudes toward money. Before it, I always calculated my expense budgetly converting to RMB. Humbly I only do haricut myself, as a symbol before landing a job (with respect to Enlai Zhou’s beard). not willing to pay extra tips nor I trust the barber with different backgrounds. After it, I pivot more onto making money instead of saving it. I started from VOO into the US stock market to outrun inflation thanks to my newly-issued SSN. I also started picking and preparing gifts for others back home. Suggested by my girlfriend and Apple gift card from my new Macbook Pro deal, I chose official websites instead of unauthorized vendors for SKIMS clothes. Frankly speaking, this trend resonates with my shifts from a student to a worker, getting deeper entangled with the society fabric from conflicting perspectives, seeking for feasible solutions to stablize it at the moment. Apart from pursuing saints from heros far away in histories, I also check more on modern lives of all kinds of people, which hides the underlying indicators of the global future. On the next section, let’s switch to an observer, capturing the telltale clues of people living by.

Live By

In spite of American’s emphasis on individual heronism, the real life looks not so fancy. I mean, even from the perspective of an observer. Indeed, UCLA campus is gorgeous as shown in the online pictures. But people within are much less perfect. Professors braging about past experiences neglecting students’ ongoing hardship while maintaining a respectable impression. TAs are responsive and helpful while irrigated by their monotonous lab affairs. Students varing much from an array of backgrounds take what they have for granted while slightly numb and confusing for what they want. Navy newbiees hang out in uniforms smingling into the cozy of Anapolis village. Police cars swing by agilely guarding the sternness of JHU Baltimore. The staff works nicely and friendly except when participating in a strike. Construtors are yelling and teasing at each other during lunch beside the fence while smiling with sweats flowing. Each single soul is encouraged free, breeding milestones from regular rountines. Groups are separated by imperceptible barriers while peacefully enjoying the same sunshine under the same sky.

Out of the university, the size is even more scaled up. Enthnic groups gather naturally, holding onto their traditions internally while trying to fashion the American style externally. American born youths celebrates their party wildly against the beach with the sunset. Europeans follow their aristocratic style in a pretty slow mindset. Mexician Chicano workers plumb around premises playing their rhythmic music. East Asians tend to be reserved by default but compete laboring meticulously. Indians and Arabs are always thought funny due to their mysterious stories. Latinos and Africans are the most optimistic, taking everything for fun, probably because they never desire too much. Jews impress both sides of curry and wise imprinted by diasopora . It’s normal to feel pretty different with different people since USA is the biggest melting pot in the world. It takes in everything and isolates its core respectively. As long as we respect the most common rules of the society, we are all welcome by all the others.

Due to the mainstream propoganda of Chinese media, I used to admire American’s creativity only. However, nothing comes out of nowhere. Innovation is positively correlated to heterogeneous communications. It’s exactly the delicate structure design from generations of fathers, pioneers, and architects that push the trends of social progress, which entwines creativity as a whole. As people within this whole, nonetheless, are not guaranteed to be like the whole, which contradicts the diversity within. Since any subjective freedom is secured, there forms objective constraints from each subjects striving to maximaize their benifits, reaching a complicated dynamical balance. While nature abhor a vacuum, customers abhor a monopolly. Once identified and tagged, giants could be sacrificed and fueled for the whole system. Or, a subject might achieve what is desired for decades, only to find it is not as expected. In this way, the returning could be no less transformative than the coming. Just like Oddessius in the Greece myth, no matter how struggling, the fate is determined by some celestial power that can’t be disobeyed.

With respect to Amerincan constitution, everyone is created equal but endowed unique. From a Darwinian perspective, the whole system keeps going stronger but keeps eliminates weakers in a gneral sense. The solution to this is whether to involution or to detach from system. While China prefers the former, the US spares for the latter. Recognized as a developed country, people aren’t definitively oriented by wealth. Sports function as totem symboling leadership and toughness. Amateur astronomers share with visitors for over 4 decades. Indeed, the wealthy pays much for the lawns, they donate much as well for social causes like education and communities.

Admitedly, I try to be seek a sense of belonging or assimilation. But that turns out to be the most false narrative of the US. In spite of the spatial proximity, people are more subject to the temporal proximity to their distinctive backgrounds from dilemmas of past generations, therefore separated by apperant skin colors and unvisible inner shields. However, as far as I am concerned, the nationality is going to dissolve elegantly as there are more and more people wandering about boundaries, bridging the gap between once enemies. They are sure to merge with new political influence and reshape the populational dynamic in the coming ages. Actually there’s one word “Chimerism”, once a force to be reckoned with, the temporary definition has shed much of its Hellentic mythical terror. Nowadays, the term is used to describe beings of mixed genetic composition – a biological phenomenon otherwise known as “chimerism”. There isn’t a essential definition of Amerinca. But everyone living here is qualified to define it by living itself. In this way, it’s nonsense to compare with others living by. The real issue we need to take seriously is to live on ourselves.

Live On

Before living on the next stage of my life. I need to discuss the elephant in the room that I deminished in the past 2 sections and last 3 articles is my near future. I just try not to describe too much about the intransigient work but in futile results. Cause any human endeavor pales in the face of the history turned full circle. Any job within ones capability won’t vary too much due to market efficiency.Any work is less meaningful as it sounds to be. The appealing action would be to derive a smooth transition from it.

Like I said about the dilemmas of the past, in fact, each age features its own dilemma. Our predecessors trade off the pros and cons, setting off or stay for better life they believe. And there’s no way back. I don’t view this unfair or in hate, since even American green cards can’t enrich the spirital crisis and struggle as well, So we’d better escape from the hesitation bewteen a humble position in the US or a brutal potential back home China and dive deeper into this dilemma itself.

In my mind, the ponderance of this dilemma alone gives authenticity to you, the reader. I found my own answer to this, and I shall share it with you. I think the courage to give meaning, even when alone, is my answer to authenticity.

I think there’s a preciousness to the ability to borrow courage from cultural narratives I’ve learned; it is the ability to assign meaning even when desperate, it is the power to have purpose even when alone.

By the poolside, I realized that my old narratives could no longer shield myself from isolation. After a life spent contorting myself to the dreams and narratives of others, I could hide no more. And so, I seized the narrative of my life giving my own meaning to it before it was too late. Even if secured a job offer, my future rests on heavy uncertainty. There is no telling how my parents would have reacted, and even less telling of what kind of future I would have built for myself. my leap is symbolic of this new choice, but also of the latency in this new found courage. But even from the fledgling courage and the short life I led, I gifted me a new understanding of authenticity.

When I look around others all over the world, I realize how little value I gave to the cultural narratives that had perpetually supported me. To live meaningfully is not merely to endure mission, but to possess the capacity to frame it. We are shaped by where we grow, affected by who we know, and avaraged by what we know before. That’s the underlying gene we can’t mask and the moot point implanted in our deep voice. Even if I deviate purposely, I find myself anchored back to it. In this case, why not accept it. To borrow courage, to applaud effort, and to situate oneself within stories larger than the present moment. That capacity, I now see, is not guaranteed. It must be cultivated, protected, and sometimes chosen against convenience, preserved with effort.

In a world increasingly shaped by visibility, performance, and legibility, authenticity is a quality under-appreciated. Yet it survives quietly, inwardly, resistant to easy interpretation. Like effort, value, and meaning, it is the internal world that strives against our label filled exterior. Like blades of grass, this is the greenery we hope to carry, not because the world notices it, but because we know it is there. If I taught me anything, it is to appreciate an authentic, real me, with all its flaws and imperfections. It is a privilege to put your own meanings in life, and it is a privilege to express it, in the way I do. Meaning does not need to be overt to be real. On the contrary, it is precisely because it cannot be easily read, measured, or displayed that it becomes authentic: an interior subtitle running beneath the surface of our lives.

Remember the whispers in your ear; remember the blade of grass.

Perhaps drawn by a call of destiny, I got the only offer as a quant SDE in Wuhan supported by my undergraduate university. At the beginning, I am kind of surprising and disappointing. But later I got my hands on and reintegrate finace into my knowledge base, which plays an indispensible role in modern economy and civilization evolution. As for personal development, it makes sense to get myself familiar with money itself and the interpersonal skills afflited with the workspace. Only then can I sophisticatedly manage money and properly use money during the ever-increasing life expectancy.

Seemingly we are doomed to regret sometime about the past. But in fact it’s the our specific missing opportunity to be blame. Never mind, the world doesn’t stop without me. Why not making peace with ourselves and let others take the charge at the moment. Technology isn’t blessed with any supermacy. In fact, tech industry is prone to build the most complex moat cause it’s hard to set valid entry threshold. If God give you a lemon, make a lemonade. Don’t get stuck there. Stay hungry stay follish and run for what is ahead. Maybe you would converge back to where you thought before in the future. As people jobs stay shorter while lives stay longer, people are pushed to be versatile and acts as entrepreneurial entity. You only live once so paint it as the owner. In the end, each one of us will leave something that gets acknowledged by the decessends.

To wrap up, I’d like to revert back to the most revered father of the USA, Benjamin Franklin, who is the only famous founding father of American I missed. (Most of his legacy remains in Philladephia, which I can’t make it due to limited time budget. Washington DC is spared for other president memorials such as George Washington, Thomas Jefferson, Abraham Lincoln, Franklin Roosevolt). Benjamin Franklin’s life embodies the American Dream. Rising from poverty to achieve success through his own efforts, he remained deeply engaged with and dedicated to serving society. In his later years, his chronicle The Autobiography of Benjamin Franklin. This book has inspired generations of Americans for over two centuries; rich in profound insights into life and timeless moral truths regarding virtue and beauty, it is widely recognized as a seminal work on the American spirit that has transformed countless lives. I am not saying there is less chance to realize the American dream. Frankly speaking, dream is just the north star and we are the sailers. The key is to take action and improve quickly in set directions no matter it’s decided fully by ones willing. No need to doubt the effectiveness. As those who aim at perfect writing by imitating the engraved copies, tho’they never reach the wish’d-for excellence of those copies, their hand is mended by the endeavor, and is tolerable while it continues fair and legible.

Good luck to myself, starting to enter the social mechanism officially means full independence but responsibility together. Remember all the principles and models verified in the student ages and live on myself. Learning by doing, observe calmly but take action swiftly and throughly. Moreover, comply the prevaling trend and no fear to practice crazy and whisical ideas. This departure is not a permenat farewell to the US. Since I can make it second times, why can’t I make it the third time? Afterall, I am still young, full of possibilities.
